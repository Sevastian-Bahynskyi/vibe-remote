Start working from your phone

  1. Add a Claude account, complete the official sign-in on the Mac, and wait for shared setup to be ready. Add a workspace by its absolute path.
  2. Press Start a session, then choose the account, the workspace, and whether to begin a new conversation or continue an earlier one.
  3. Sign in to the same Claude account on your phone, then press Open in Claude on the session.

Running several at once

Each session is independent and gets its own name in the Claude app. Starting, stopping or deleting one never disturbs the others.

Continuing in another agent

Choose Hand off on a session to continue in another Claude account or Codex. Vibe Remote stops the source and carries its latest conversation and Git state. Another Claude account starts automatically. For Codex, open the same workspace and ask it to continue within 48 hours.

First use of a workspace

Adding a workspace authorizes managed sessions to trust that folder and enable its configured MCP servers. A missing integration blocks launch with a diagnostic. Use Repair setup under Accounts after stopping that account’s sessions to prepare missing shared components.

Models and shared memory

Continuing a conversation preserves its model and effort. New or unknown conversations use Opus with high effort. Session details show requested settings separately from the latest observation; use Change setup to choose another model and restart deliberately.

Managed accounts share learned memory for the same project, including linked worktrees. Imported differences stay separate, with provenance and a conflict index. Original notes and normal Claude memory stay untouched.

Setup and refresh

Accounts keeps setup progress through sign-in and preparation. Cancel, retry, or repair an interrupted setup there. Refresh works on every screen and preserves unsaved forms. Closing an approval console disconnects its polling; it does not stop the session.

Staying reachable

The Mac must be awake and on the tailnet. On mains power Vibe Remote holds off idle sleep while a session is serving; on battery it does not, and closing the lid disconnects either way.
