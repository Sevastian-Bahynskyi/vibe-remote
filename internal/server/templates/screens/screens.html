Start working from your phone

  1. Add a Claude account and a workspace.
  2. Press Start a session, then choose the account, the workspace, and whether to begin a new conversation or continue an earlier one.
  3. Sign in to the same Claude account on your phone, then press Open in Claude on the session.

Running several at once

Each session is independent and gets its own name in the Claude app. Starting, stopping or deleting one never disturbs the others.

Moving a session

Change account, workspace or conversation restarts the session with the new settings. A conversation belongs to the account and workspace it started in, so moving a session elsewhere begins a new one.

Continuing in another agent

Open a conversation, choose where to continue it, then open that agent in the same workspace and type exactly continue. The handoff carries the last few turns plus the current Git state, and lasts 48 hours.

First use of a workspace

Claude asks once, locally, whether it may work in a folder. Run Claude on the Mac in that folder with the chosen account and accept the prompt. Vibe Remote reports this rather than bypassing it.

Staying reachable

The Mac must be awake and on the tailnet. On mains power Vibe Remote holds off idle sleep while a session is serving; on battery it does not, and closing the lid disconnects either way.
